Organize PDF Pages

Rearrange, rotate, copy and remove pages visually. Nothing is uploaded.

How to organize PDF pages

  1. Choose a PDF and wait for the page thumbnails to appear.
  2. Drag a page to a new position, or use the buttons under it to rotate (⟳), duplicate (⧉) or delete (✕) it.
  3. Use “Blank page” to add an empty page at the end.
  4. Click Save PDF to download the new version.

Thumbnails are generated one by one, so long documents take a little while to load.
