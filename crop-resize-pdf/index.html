Crop & Resize PDF

Cut away page margins and, if you like, fit every page to A4 or Letter. Nothing is uploaded.

How to crop a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Enter how much to remove from the top, right, bottom and left, in points. 72 points equal 1 inch, so 36 is half an inch.
  3. Optional: choose A4 or Letter to resize every page after cropping.
  4. Click Apply & download.

The same crop is applied to every page. There is no drag-to-crop preview yet, so try a small value first and check the result.
