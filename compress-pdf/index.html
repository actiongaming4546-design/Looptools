Compress PDF

Reduce the size of a PDF so it is easier to email or upload. Your file never leaves your device.

How to compress a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Pick Light, Balanced or Maximum. Stronger levels make smaller files but lower the image quality.
  3. Click Compress & download and compare the before and after sizes.

This method turns every page into a compressed image. That works well for scanned or photo-heavy PDFs, but text in the result can no longer be selected or searched. If the result is not smaller than the original, nothing is downloaded, so try a stronger level.
