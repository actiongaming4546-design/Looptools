About

Looptools (Small tasks. Sorted.) is a free set of PDF tools that run in your browser. You do not need an account, and files are processed on your own device rather than uploaded by this site.

It is built with open-source libraries: pdf-lib (MIT), PDF.js (Apache-2.0) and JSZip (MIT or GPL-3.0, used here under MIT).

Browse the tools or read the privacy page.
