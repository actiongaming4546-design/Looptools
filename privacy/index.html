Privacy

Your files. The tools process files in your browser. This site’s code does not upload your files or their names anywhere.

Network requests. The home page and information pages load only this site’s own files. Each tool page also loads three open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js and JSZip) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Like any web request, this lets that provider see standard details such as your IP address and browser type. It does not receive your files.

Stored in your browser. Your light or dark theme choice and, if you save one in Edit & Sign, your signature image are kept in your browser’s local storage on your device. We do not set cookies.

Hosting. The site is hosted on Render, which may keep standard server logs of page requests. Those logs never include the contents of your files, because the files are not sent.

Analytics and ads. None are used at the moment. If that changes, this page will be updated to say what is collected.
