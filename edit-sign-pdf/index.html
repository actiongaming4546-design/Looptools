Edit & Sign PDF

Fill in a form, mark up a page or sign a document. Your file and your signature stay on your device.

How to sign or edit a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF. The first page appears.
  2. Draw a signature in the box, or type your name, then click Save signature.
  3. Pick a tool: Text, Whiteout, Highlight, Draw or Signature, then click or drag on the page. Use the arrows to change page, and Undo or Redo if you slip.
  4. Click Save PDF to download the edited file.

Edits are added on top of the page. Whiteout covers content with a white box but does not delete the text underneath, so do not rely on it to hide sensitive information. Added text uses a standard font with Latin characters only. A saved signature is stored in this browser only.
