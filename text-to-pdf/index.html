Text → PDF

Paste or type text and get a simple, readable PDF. Nothing is uploaded.

How to convert text to PDF

  1. Type or paste your text, or load a .txt file.
  2. Choose A4 or Letter and a font size.
  3. Click Create PDF. Long lines wrap and the text continues onto new pages automatically.

The tool uses a standard font that supports Latin letters, numbers and common punctuation. Characters outside that set, such as Hindi, Arabic or Chinese, are replaced with a question mark.
